## Data

- Best-known ranks in `problems/known.json` and `problems/catalog.json` are facts taken from
  Sedoglavic's [catalogue of fast matrix multiplication algorithms](https://fmm.univ-lille.fr/), cited
  with their source and date wherever they are shown.
- Strassen's algorithm (`app/public/samples/strassen-2x2x2.json`) is from V. Strassen, *Gaussian
  elimination is not optimal*, Numerische Mathematik 13 (1969).
